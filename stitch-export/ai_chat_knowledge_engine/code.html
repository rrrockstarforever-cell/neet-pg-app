A 62-year-old male with septic shock secondary to acute necrotizing pancreatitis in the ICU develops bacteremia. Blood cultures grow Klebsiella pneumoniae resistant to all carbapenems, extended-spectrum cephalosporins, and fluoroquinolones. Aztreonam + Ceftazidime-avibactam combination Correct Key
tips_and_updates Board Pearl • Ambler Class B Metallo-Enzyme Traps

NDM-1, VIM, & IMP are zinc-dependent metallo-β-lactamases (Ambler Class B). They hydrolyze all carbapenems and β-lactams except aztreonam. However, clinical strains co-produce ESBLs or AmpC that hydrolyze aztreonam. Pairing Aztreonam + Avibactam (from CAZ-AVI) protects aztreonam from companion ESBLs while aztreonam evades the NDM metallo-carbapenemase!
