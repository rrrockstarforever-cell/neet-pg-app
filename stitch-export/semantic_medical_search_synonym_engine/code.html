"Acquired vancomycin resistance in Enterococci involves bacterial cell wall modifications where terminal pentapeptide binding affinity drops by >1,000 fold. Vancomycin typically binds the D-alanyl-D-alanine terminus of cell wall peptidoglycan precursors, inhibiting cell-wall transglycosylation. Replacement with D-alanyl-D-lactate abolishes the critical hydrogen bond required for antibiotic complex stabilization."
